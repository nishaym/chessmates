# chessmates
Repo to document the journey of the ChessMates club

My chess journey began at about 8 years old, when my sister and I learned to play from my father. The three of us would play together, and even though I didn't enjoy it at first, I would eventually start to love playing. I played any time I could, with my parents, my sister, my grandparents, and my cousins. In fourth grade I discovered that many of my friends also liked chess, so I began playing with them. One of my close friends I would play against was Daniel Joseph, who now, years later, is a founding member in this club!
